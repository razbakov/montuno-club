# Montuno Club — Branding Analysis

## Context

Alex runs two related activities under "Montuno Club":
1. **Dance classes** — Salsa Cubana classes (Sundays at Buena Vista bar)
2. **Parties** — Saturday night salsa events at various venues

The question: keep "Montuno Club" for both, or split the brand?

## What is a "Montuno"?

A **montuno** is the rhythmic, call-and-response section of Cuban son music — the part where the energy builds, the dancers come alive, and the party really starts. It's a strong, authentic name rooted in Cuban music culture.

## Analysis

### Option A: Keep "Montuno Club" for everything

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Brand recognition already built (750+ Instagram followers, montunoclub.de domain) | "Club" can confuse venues — sounds like a competitor, not a partner |
| Single brand = simpler marketing | Classes and parties attract different audiences with different expectations |
| Community knows the name | Harder to pitch party partnerships if the name sounds like a dance school |

### Option B: Split — "Montuno Club" for classes, new name for parties

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Venues see a party brand, not a dance school looking for space | Two brands to maintain = double the marketing effort |
| Can tailor messaging: class brand = learning, party brand = nightlife | Splits the existing audience |
| Party brand can grow independently | Loses the 750+-follower head start |

### Option C (Recommended): "Montuno Club" for everything, with sub-labels

Keep **Montuno Club** as the umbrella brand but use descriptive sub-labels:

- **Montuno Club Classes** — for the teaching side (Sundays)
- **Montuno Club Nights** or **Montuno Nights** — for the party side (Saturdays)

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Leverages existing brand equity (Instagram, domain, community recognition) | Slightly more complex messaging than a single flat brand |
| Clear differentiation without brand fragmentation | — |
| "Montuno Nights" sounds like a party, not a school — easier venue pitch | — |
| One Instagram account, one website, one community | — |
| Venues see "Montuno Nights" on the flyer, not "Montuno Club dance school" | — |

## Recommendation

**Keep "Montuno Club" as the master brand. Use "Montuno Nights" for party events.**

Reasons:
1. **Don't throw away what you've built.** Over 750 followers, a domain, and community name recognition are real assets. Starting from zero with a new brand costs time and money.
2. **"Montuno Nights" solves the venue problem.** When pitching to N.V. Club or Max Emanuel, "Montuno Nights presents: Salsa Cubana Saturday" reads as an event, not a school asking for space.
3. **One community, two experiences.** Students from classes become party-goers. Party-goers discover classes. Cross-pollination only works if both live under one roof.
4. **Simple execution.** One Instagram bio: "Salsa Cubana in Munich | Classes Sundays | Parties Saturdays". One website with two sections. One mailing list.

## Implementation

- **Instagram bio:** "@montunoclub — Salsa Cubana Munich | Classes (Sun) | Montuno Nights (Sat)"
- **Website:** Add a "Montuno Nights" section to montunoclub.de with party schedule, venue info, photo gallery
- **Flyers/posters:** Use "Montuno Nights" as the event brand, with "by Montuno Club" in smaller text
- **Email signatures:** Use "Montuno Club" for class communications, "Montuno Nights" for party/venue communications
- **Venue pitches:** Lead with "Montuno Nights" — it sounds like an event series, which is exactly what venues want to host

## Name alternatives considered

If Alex wants to explore beyond "Montuno Nights":

| Name | Vibe | Risk |
|------|------|------|
| **Montuno Nights** | Authentic, clear, party-ready | None — strong pick |
| **Noche Montuno** | Spanish flair, sounds exotic | May confuse German audience |
| **Son & Soul** | Poetic, references Cuban son | Loses Montuno brand connection |
| **La Rumba Munich** | High energy, Cuban | Generic — many "Rumba" events exist |
| **Club Montuno** | Flip of current name | Still sounds like a venue/club |

**Verdict:** "Montuno Nights" is the strongest option. It's clear, memorable, party-appropriate, and preserves the brand.
