# Montuno Club — Marketing Strategy

**Date:** 2026-03-24
**Status:** Draft for review

## 1. Branding Decision

### Recommendation: Dual Brand Architecture

| Brand | Purpose | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| **Montuno Club** | Master brand — dance classes, community, identity | Existing students, WeDance ecosystem |
| **Montuno Nights** | Event sub-brand — Saturday parties, venue nights | Broader Munich nightlife audience |

**Why this works:**
- Preserves the existing 750+ Instagram following and `montunoclub.de` domain
- "Montuno Nights" signals event/party — not a school — making venue pitches easier
- One community, two touchpoints: learn on Sundays, party on Saturdays
- Consistent visual identity, two distinct hooks depending on context

- [ ] Or choose a fully separate party brand (higher cost: new accounts, new domain)

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary: Montuno Nights Party Audience
- Age 25–40, Munich
- Social dancers at all levels (not just Montuno students)
- People who enjoy Latin nights, looking for something beyond standard clubs
- Couples and friend groups who want an experience, not just a bar

### Secondary: Montuno Club Classes Audience
- Beginners curious about Salsa Cubana
- People from the Montuno Nights parties who want to learn
- WeDance platform users in Munich

## 3. Marketing Channels

### Instagram (@montunoclub)
- **Current:** 750+ followers, active community
- **Strategy:** Dual content pillars
  - Classes content: tutorials, student progress, class atmosphere
  - Party content: event announcements, recap videos, DJ/music previews
- **Cadence:** 3–4 posts/week + Stories daily on event weeks
- **Reels:** Event highlight reels perform best — 30s clips from party floor

### WhatsApp Community
- Primary channel for event announcements and last-minute updates
- Segment into: Classes group + Party announcements group
- Add venue partners to the announcements group (builds relationship)

### Flyers & Physical Presence
- At Meneate festival: distribute cards/flyers for upcoming Montuno Nights
- At dance schools (Salsa Munich ecosystem): physical flyers allowed?
- At N.V. Club / Max Emanuel: leave cards with bartenders after event launch

### Word of Mouth / Community
- Milo and key community figures as ambassadors — tell them first, let them spread
- "Bring a friend" mechanic: +1 always free on first Saturday

### WeDance Platform
- List all events on WeDance — feeds into the broader ecosystem
- Montuno Club profile as a discovery channel for new dancers

## 7. Content Strategy (Instagram)

### Content Pillars

**1. Community & Atmosphere (40%)**
- Photos/videos from classes and parties
- Student spotlights
- Behind-the-scenes

**2. Education (30%)**
- Short Salsa Cubana tutorials (Reels)
- "Did you know?" about Cuban dance culture
- Music recommendations

**3. Events & Announcements (20%)**
- Upcoming events, "next Saturday" reminders
- Venue partnerships
- Festival appearances (Meneate etc.)

**4. Social Proof (10%)**
- Testimonials from students
- Venue/partner tags
- Community milestones (follower count, event attendance records)
