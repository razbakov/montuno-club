# Experiment: Instagram-Driven Discovery

**Requirement:** `logbook/requirements/beginners-find-class.md`
**Date:** 2026-04-05
**Status:** Proposed

## Hypothesis

We believe that **posting 3x/week on Instagram (1 reel + 2 feed posts) with Munich-targeted hashtags** will result in **at least 3 new attendees per month discovering the class through Instagram** for **salsa beginners in Munich's expat community**.

## Method

1. Content & Growth Lead creates content calendar with 3 posts/week cadence — Week 1
2. Alex provides raw photos/videos after each Sunday class — Ongoing
3. Content & Growth Lead drafts content, Alex approves and publishes — Weekly cycle
4. Track "How did you find us?" at each class — Ongoing
5. Review Instagram Insights monthly — Content & Growth Lead reports

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | How Measured |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| New attendees citing Instagram | 3+/month | "How did you find us?" at class |
| Instagram followers | 200 in 3 months | Instagram Insights |
| Post engagement rate | >5% | Likes + comments + shares / followers |
| Posting consistency | 3x/week achieved 80%+ of weeks | Content log |

## Pivot Triggers

If any of the following are true at the 3-month evaluation:

- Fewer than 5 total new attendees cited Instagram over 3 months
- Engagement rate below 2% despite consistent posting
- Alex can't sustain providing raw material (bottleneck)

## Persevere Criteria

All of the following must be true to continue on this path:

- [ ] At least 3 new attendees/month citing Instagram by month 3
- [ ] Engagement rate above 3%
- [ ] Content pipeline is sustainable (Alex provides material, Content & Growth Lead drafts, approval within 48h)
- [ ] Instagram followers growing month-over-month

## Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Alex doesn't provide raw material consistently | M | H | Create template for quick phone photos; lower the bar for "good enough" raw material |
| Low organic reach | H | M | Experiment with Reels (higher reach); engage with Munich expat accounts; cross-post to expat Facebook groups |
| Content feels inauthentic | L | H | Content approval policy ensures Alex validates every post; authentic-over-aspirational principle |
