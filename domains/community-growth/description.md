# Community & Growth — Domain Description (Delegation Canvas)

**Date/Version:** 2026-04-05
**Delegator:** Alex (Instructor & Founder)
**Review date:** 2026-07-05

## Purpose
**Primary Driver:** People searching for salsa classes in Munich encounter paid studio listings and German-language results. Montuno Club doesn't surface in their search. Returning attendees lose momentum between weekly sessions without community engagement.
**Main Requirement:** Make Montuno Club discoverable online and keep the community engaged between classes so that attendance grows and retention improves.

## Key Responsibilities
- Create and publish social media content (Instagram reels, stories, posts)
- Write email campaigns and event announcements
- Optimize WeDance event descriptions for search
- Produce class recap content (highlights, photos, quotes)
- Monitor engagement metrics and adjust content strategy
- Draft copy for any promotional materials

## Customers and Deliverables
| Customer | Deliverable |
|----------|-------------|
| Potential attendees (discovery) | Instagram content, optimized event listings, shareable posts |
| Current community (retention) | Class recaps, event reminders, community highlights |
| Alex (delegator) | Content calendar, engagement reports, growth recommendations |

## Dependencies
| Provider | What they deliver |
|----------|-------------------|
| Teaching domain (Alex) | Class photos/videos, curriculum highlights, event details |
| WeDance.vip | Event platform and SEO |
| Instagram | Content distribution platform |

## External Constraints
- All content must be approved by Alex before publishing
- No budget for paid advertising
- Content must reflect the actual class vibe (authentic, not aspirational)
- Must comply with Instagram/platform terms of service

## Key Challenges
- Content creation depends on raw material (photos/videos) from Alex
- Organic social media reach is unpredictable
- Maintaining consistent posting cadence without paid tools

## Key Resources
- Class photos and videos (provided by Alex)
- Instagram account
- WeDance event platform
- Email list (as it grows)

## Delegator Responsibilities
- Provide raw content (photos, videos, quotes) after each class
- Review and approve content before publication
- Share access to Instagram and WeDance accounts
- Provide feedback on content quality and brand alignment

## Competencies, Qualities, and Skills
- Social media copywriting (Instagram-native tone)
- Understanding of dance community culture
- SEO basics for event descriptions
- Visual storytelling
- Email marketing fundamentals

## Key Metrics and Monitoring

| Metric | How Measured | Frequency | Measured By |
|--------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| Instagram followers | Instagram Insights | Monthly | Content & Growth Lead |
| Post engagement rate | Likes + comments + shares / followers | Monthly | Content & Growth Lead |
| New attendees from online | "How did you find us?" at class | Monthly | Alex |
| Email open rate | Email platform analytics | Per campaign | Content & Growth Lead |

## Evaluation Schedule
Quarterly review by Alex against metrics. First evaluation: 2026-07-05.
