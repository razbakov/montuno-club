# Teaching — Domain Description (Delegation Canvas)

**Date/Version:** 2026-04-05
**Delegator:** Alex (self-delegated — founder is also the instructor)
**Review date:** 2026-07-05

## Purpose
**Primary Driver:** First-timers at dance events feel intimidated and don't return if the experience is overwhelming. The class venue arrangement depends on an informal relationship with the bar.
**Main Requirement:** Deliver a consistent, welcoming weekly class that turns curious beginners into returning dancers, while maintaining a stable venue.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design and deliver the weekly class curriculum (one move per session, partner rotation, social dancing)
- Create a welcoming environment where first-timers feel included
- Maintain the venue relationship with Buena Vista Bar
- Manage the WeDance event listing and registration
- Scout backup venues as contingency

## Customers and Deliverables
| Customer | Deliverable |
|----------|-------------|
| Beginners | A fun, structured 60-min class every Sunday at 7 PM |
| Returning dancers | Progressive curriculum that builds on previous weeks |
| Buena Vista Bar | Consistent Sunday evening foot traffic |

## Dependencies
| Provider | What they deliver |
|----------|-------------------|
| Community & Growth domain | New attendees through content and promotion |
| WeDance.vip | Registration platform and event visibility |
| Buena Vista Bar | Venue space and sound system |

## External Constraints
- Bar closing time limits class duration
- No formal rental agreement — relationship-dependent
- Instructor availability (Alex is the only teacher)

## Key Challenges
- Single point of failure (one instructor)
- No backup venue currently confirmed
- Attendance varies with weather, holidays, competing events

## Key Resources
- Alex's teaching experience (7 years, trained in Cuba)
- Buena Vista Bar space (free)
- Personal sound equipment (if needed)
- WeDance platform (free)

## Delegator Responsibilities
Self-delegated. Alex is accountable to the community for consistency and quality.

## Competencies, Qualities, and Skills
- Salsa Cubana (Casino) instruction experience
- Ability to teach in English to mixed-level, multicultural groups
- Social skills to make newcomers feel welcome
- Basic event management

## Key Metrics and Monitoring

| Metric | How Measured | Frequency | Measured By |
|--------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| Weekly attendance | WeDance registrations + head count | Weekly | Alex |
| Retention rate | Returning faces over 4-week window | Monthly | Alex |
| First-timer return rate | New attendees who come back within 2 weeks | Monthly | Alex |

## Evaluation Schedule
Quarterly self-review against metrics. First evaluation: 2026-07-05.
