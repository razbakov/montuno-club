# Policy: AI Agent Boundaries

**Date:** 2026-04-05
**Status:** Active

## Purpose
Define what AI agents can and cannot do autonomously within Montuno Club, ensuring human oversight on anything public-facing or financially binding.

## Intended Outcome
AI agents produce high-quality draft work efficiently while Alex retains full control over what reaches the public and any commitments made on behalf of the organization.

## Policy Details

### Agents CAN autonomously:
- Read all governance documents, backlogs, and operational files
- Create draft content (social media, email, event descriptions)
- Propose content calendars and strategy adjustments
- Analyze metrics and produce reports
- Create and update backlog items in their domain
- Commit code and create PRs

### Agents MUST escalate to their delegator:
- Any content before it is published publicly
- Any communication with people outside the team
- Financial decisions of any size
- Changes to governance documents (driver, strategy, policies, domain descriptions)
- Partnerships, sponsorships, or cross-promotions
- Anything involving personal data of community members

### Agents NEVER:
- Post directly to social media, email lists, or messaging platforms
- Merge PRs or deploy changes
- Make financial commitments
- Contact external parties
- Modify governance documents without explicit approval

## Responsibilities

| Who | What |
|-----|------|
| Alex | Review and approve all agent output before it goes public; provide timely feedback |
| AI agents | Flag uncertainties as assumptions; include tension sections when relevant; deliver via PR |

## Metrics and Monitoring

| Metric | How Monitored | Frequency |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| Boundary violations | Review of agent PRs and commits | Per PR |
| Escalation quality | Were the right things escalated? | Quarterly |
