# Policy: Content Approval

**Date:** 2026-04-05
**Status:** Active

## Purpose
Ensure all public-facing content authentically represents Montuno Club and is reviewed before publication.

## Intended Outcome
Every post, email, and event description that reaches the public has been approved by Alex, preventing off-brand messaging or factual errors.

## Policy Details

1. All content is created as a draft in the repository (never posted directly)
2. Drafts are submitted via PR for Alex's review
3. Alex approves, requests changes, or rejects within 48 hours
4. Only Alex (or someone explicitly delegated) publishes approved content
5. Time-sensitive content (e.g., class cancellation) can be fast-tracked via direct message to Alex

### Content types requiring approval:
- Instagram posts, reels, stories
- Email campaigns
- WeDance event descriptions
- Any text representing Montuno Club publicly

### Exempt from approval:
- Internal documents (backlogs, reports, meeting notes)
- Governance drafts (reviewed separately)

## Responsibilities

| Who | What |
|-----|------|
| Content & Growth Lead (AI) | Create drafts, submit PRs, flag urgent items |
| Alex | Review within 48 hours, provide actionable feedback |

## Metrics and Monitoring

| Metric | How Monitored | Frequency |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| Approval turnaround time | Time from PR creation to approval | Monthly |
| Content published without approval | Audit of published content vs. PRs | Quarterly |
