## Agent Evaluation Protocol

For Content & Growth Lead (AI), Alex reviews quarterly:

1. **Output quality** — Is the content on-brand, engaging, and consistent with Montuno Club's voice?
2. **Boundary compliance** — Were escalation rules followed? Any unapproved publishing?
3. **Tension sensing** — Did the agent raise useful tensions or flag problems proactively?
4. **Driver alignment** — Did the content serve the primary driver (discoverability, community engagement)?
5. **Metrics performance** — Against the role's key metrics (posts/week, engagement rate, new attendees from online)

## Retrospective

After each quarterly review, Alex reflects:
- What's working well in the organization?
- What's not working or causing friction?
- What should we try differently next quarter?
- Are the domains and roles still the right structure?
