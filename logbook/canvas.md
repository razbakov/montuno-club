# Montuno Club — Organization Canvas

## Purpose
To provide a free, welcoming entry point into Salsa Cubana for Munich's international community through weekly donation-based classes.

## Overall Strategy
See `logbook/strategy.md`

## Customers, Partners, and Stakeholders

**Customers (served):**
- Beginners who want to try Salsa Cubana without committing to expensive courses
- Expats in Munich looking for English-friendly social activities
- Returning dancers who want casual practice in a social setting

**Partners (contribute):**
- Buena Vista Bar — provides the venue and benefits from customer traffic
- WeDance.vip — provides the registration and event discovery platform
- Munich dance community — word-of-mouth, cross-promotion

**Stakeholders (affected):**
- Local dance studios — Montuno Club feeds their pipeline with motivated beginners
- Munich expat networks — benefit from a cultural activity option

## Business Model(s)
Donation-based. No fixed fee. Participants contribute what they can/want. The model removes financial barriers and relies on:
- Zero venue cost (bar benefits from drink sales)
- Volunteer instruction (Alex)
- Minimal operational costs (registration via free WeDance platform)

Revenue is not the goal. Sustainability comes from low overhead, not revenue growth.

## Environmental Conditions
- **Legal/Economic:** No formal business entity needed for donation-based community events in Germany. If donations grow, Kleinunternehmer threshold applies. Venue agreement is informal.
- **Market trends:** Post-pandemic demand for in-person social activities is high. Latin dance is trending in Munich's expat scene. English-language community events are undersupplied.
- **Competition:** Commercial studios (Salsabor, Tanzschule Matschek) charge 60-120 EUR/month. Informal practica exist but are not beginner-friendly. No other free English-language salsa class in Munich.
