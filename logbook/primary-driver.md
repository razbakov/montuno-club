# Primary Driver and Main Requirement

## Primary Driver

Salsa Cubana beginners in Munich's international community rely on expensive studio classes taught in German, while casual learning happens in closed social circles that newcomers can't access. Many potential dancers — especially expats — never start because they can't find an affordable, welcoming, English-speaking entry point. Without an accessible gateway, the local salsa community misses fresh energy and stays smaller than it could be.

---

## Main Requirement

### Intended Outcomes
- A steady flow of new dancers joining the Munich salsa community each month
- Beginners feeling welcome enough to return after their first class and progress into the broader dance scene
- A self-sustaining community gathering that doesn't depend on commercial studio economics

### Enabling Conditions
- A free, donation-based class running weekly at a consistent time and place
- English as the default language to include the expat community
- A social venue (bar, not studio) that lowers the intimidation threshold
- Online visibility that reaches people searching for dance classes in Munich
- Registration through an established dance platform (WeDance.vip)
