# Requirement: Community Retention

**Actor:** Returning dancers
**Domain:** Community & Growth

## Purpose

### Driver
Attendees who come once or twice lose momentum between sessions. Without reminders, event recaps, or a sense of belonging to a group, they drift away — especially if they don't yet have dance partners or friends in the community.

### Requirement
**Returning dancers** need ongoing engagement between classes (recaps, event announcements, community content) so that they stay connected and keep coming back.

## Priority
Medium

## Type
Operations

## Notes
Channels: Instagram stories (class recaps, highlights), email updates, WhatsApp group (if critical mass reached). Content should make people feel part of something, not just informed.
