# Requirement: Beginners Can Find the Class

**Actor:** Salsa beginners in Munich (especially expats)
**Domain:** Community & Growth

## Purpose

### Driver
People searching for salsa classes in Munich encounter paid studio listings and German-language results. Montuno Club's free English class doesn't surface in their search. Potential attendees who would benefit from the class never discover it exists.

### Requirement
**Beginners** need to discover Montuno Club through online search and social media so that they can attend their first class without relying on personal connections.

## Priority
High

## Type
Operations

## Notes
Key channels: Instagram (reels/stories), Google search ("salsa class Munich"), WeDance event listings, expat Facebook groups. Content must convey the vibe (social, fun, free) not just logistics.
