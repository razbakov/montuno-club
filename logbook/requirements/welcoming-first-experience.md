# Requirement: Welcoming First Experience

**Actor:** First-time attendees
**Domain:** Teaching

## Purpose

### Driver
First-timers at dance events often feel intimidated — they don't know anyone, don't know the moves, and worry about looking foolish. If their first experience is overwhelming or cliquey, they don't return.

### Requirement
**First-time attendees** need a structured, supportive first class experience so that they feel confident enough to return the following week.

## Priority
High

## Type
Operations

## Notes
Class design: one move per session, partner rotation so no one is left out, social dancing time where the instructor actively includes newcomers. The bar setting helps — people can grab a drink and ease in.
