# Requirement: Venue Sustainability

**Actor:** Buena Vista Bar (partner), Alex (founder)
**Domain:** Teaching

## Purpose

### Driver
The class runs at Buena Vista Bar with no formal rental agreement — the bar benefits from drink sales on a typically slow Sunday evening. If attendance drops or the bar changes management, the venue arrangement could end without notice.

### Requirement
**Alex** needs a stable venue arrangement so that the class can run consistently without disruption.

## Priority
Medium

## Type
Governance

## Notes
Mitigation: maintain good relationship with bar staff, ensure attendees buy drinks, have 1-2 backup venue options scouted. Consider a simple written agreement if the relationship formalizes.
