## Intended Outcome(s)
- 10+ regular weekly attendees within 3 months
- At least 3 new faces per month discovering the class through online channels
- A recognizable community identity that people associate with accessible salsa in Munich

## Strategy

**Anchor in consistency, grow through content.**

Run a high-quality, beginner-friendly class every Sunday at 7 PM at the same venue. Consistency is the product — people need to know it's always there. Growth comes from social media content (Instagram reels/stories showing the vibe, not just the steps) and WeDance event listings that surface in search. The bar setting makes it social-first: people come for the experience, stay for the dance.

Don't try to compete with studios on curriculum depth. Compete on accessibility, vibe, and community. Keep the class simple (one move per session, lots of social dancing), so first-timers never feel lost.

Rationale: Dance communities grow through visible social proof. A reel of people laughing and dancing at a bar reaches more beginners than a perfectly structured syllabus. The donation model removes the "is it worth the money?" friction entirely.
