## Purpose
**Primary Driver:** The organization needs someone to deliver the weekly class and maintain the overall vision for Montuno Club.
**Main Requirement:** Teach a consistent, high-quality beginner Salsa Cubana class every Sunday and make governance decisions for the organization.

## Key Responsibilities
- Teach the weekly class (curriculum design, instruction, partner rotation facilitation)
- Maintain venue relationship with Buena Vista Bar
- Manage WeDance event listing and registrations
- Approve all published content (social media, emails)
- Make governance decisions (strategy, policies, domain changes)
- Provide raw content (photos, videos) to the Content & Growth Lead
- Monitor attendance and community health
